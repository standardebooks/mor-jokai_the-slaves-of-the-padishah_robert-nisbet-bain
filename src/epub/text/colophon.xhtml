was published in 1852 by
Mór Jókai.
It was translated from Hungarian in 1903 by
Robert Nisbet Bain.

Standard Ebooks

Steven desJardins and Distributed Proofreaders
for
Project Gutenberg
and on digital scans from the
Internet Archive.

Combat of a Greek and a Turk,
a painting completed circa 1835 by
An Unknown Artist.

League Spartan and Sorts Mill Goudy
typefaces created in 2014 and 2009 by
The League of Moveable Type.
